Gemini API key

The extension works without a key using Google Translate. Adding a free Gemini key produces natural, conversational Hinglish instead. No credit card required.

Privacy: the text you convert is sent to Google to be translated. On the free Gemini tier, Google may use it to improve its products and human reviewers may read it. Don't convert private emails or documents with a free key.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Create API key.
  3. Copy the key and paste it below.
